### País o nacionalidad: nunca se mezclan

| *from* + país | *be* + nacionalidad |
| --- | --- |
| *I'm **from Spain**.* | *I'm **Spanish**.* |
| *He's **from Scotland**.* | *He's **Scottish**.* |
| *She's **from the USA**.* | *She's **American**.* |

- Nunca <s>*I'm from Spanish*</s> ni <s>*I'm Spain*</s>.
- Países, nacionalidades e idiomas, siempre con **mayúscula**: *Spanish*.
- El acento puede cambiar: *ITaly → iTALian*, *JaPAN → JapaNESE*.
- *the* con *the USA*, *the UK*, *the Czech Republic*.

### Preguntas

- ***Where are you from?*** (*from* al final) · ***Are you English?*** – *No, I'm not. I'm Scottish.*
- ***Where in Spain are you from?*** Ver [Situar una localidad en un país](notion-localidad.html).

### Dónde naciste

- ***I was born in** 1995 / in Zamora / in May.* · ***I was born on** the 15th of April.*
- ***Where were you born?*** · ***Were you born in Zamora?*** – *Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.*
