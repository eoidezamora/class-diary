### Días y meses

- *Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday* («wens-dei»), *Thursday, Friday* (**weekdays**) · *Saturday, Sunday* (**the weekend**).
- *January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December*.
- Siempre con **mayúscula**.
- *yesterday · today · tomorrow · the day after tomorrow* · *a bank holiday* (festivo en el Reino Unido).

### Fechas

- Con ordinales: ***on the** 27th **of** November* → *on the twenty-seventh of November*.
- *1st, 2nd, 3rd, 21st, 22nd, 23rd, 31st*; el resto *-th* (*11th, 12th, 13th*).

### Los años

| Año | Se lee |
| --- | --- |
| 1995 | *nineteen ninety-five* (de dos en dos) |
| 1907 | *nineteen **oh** seven* |
| 1900 | *nineteen **hundred*** |
| 2000 | *two **thousand*** |
| 2004 | *two thousand **and** four* |
| 2026 | *twenty twenty-six* · *two thousand and twenty-six* |

- ***in*** + año o mes (*in 1995*, *in May*) · ***on*** + fecha completa (*on the 15th of April*).
- *the nineties*, *the 1960s* (*the nineteen sixties*).
