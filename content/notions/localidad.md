### Preguntar

- ***Where are you from?*** – *I'm from Colombia.* – ***Where in Colombia?***
- *Is it in the north? · Is it to the east of Zamora? · Can you spell it, please?*

### Dentro o fuera: *in* o *to*

| | Significa | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ***in** the north of* | **dentro** de ese país o región | *A Coruña is **in** the north-west of Spain.* |
| ***to** the north of* | **fuera**, en esa dirección | *Portugal is **to** the west of Spain.* |

- *north, south, east, west* · *north-east, south-west…* (con guion) · *in the centre of*.
- Van en minúscula cuando son dirección; en mayúscula si forman parte de un nombre: *North America*, *Northern Ireland*.
- *the* /ðə/ *north* pero /ði/ *east*.

### Cerca de algo: *next to*, *near*, *by*

- ***next to*** = pegado, al lado: *Zamora is next to Portugal.*
- ***near*** = cerca: *It's near Madrid.* · *Ulan-Ude is near Lake Baikal.*
- ***by*** = a orillas de: *Zamora is **by the river** Duero.* · *London is by the Thames* /temz/.
- Los ríos llevan *the* (*the Duero*, *the Tiber*); los lagos no (*Lake Sanabria*).

### Describe tu ciudad o tu pueblo en una frase

**qué es** + **dónde está** + **qué tiene cerca**:

> *I'm from **Zamora**, **a small city** **in the north-west of Spain**, **by the river Duero**.*

- *a city · a town · a village · a seaside town*
- *on the coast · in the mountains · by the sea · at the foot of the mountains*
- Para seguir: *What's it like? – It's small and quiet.*
