| ***a* + envase + *of* + contenido** → lo que te bebes o usas | **contenido + envase** → el objeto |
| --- | --- |
| *a bottle of water* | *a water bottle* |
| *a cup of coffee* | *a coffee cup* |
| *a glass of wine* | *a wine glass* |
| *a cup of tea* | *a teacup* |
| *a box of matches* | *a matchbox* |

- Con *of*: el contenido. Sin *of*: la primera palabra funciona como adjetivo y hablamos del recipiente.
- *Can I have a bottle of water, please?* · *My water bottle is empty.*
