What's it like? Describir objetos

Tamaño Forma Color Material
big, large, small, tiny, long, short, thin, thick, heavy, light round, square, rectangular, flat, oval, triangular black, white, brown, red, yellow, grey… made of plastic, leather, metal, paper, glass, wood
  • What's it like? = ¿Cómo es? (no confundir con Do you like it?). What's it made of? = ¿De qué está hecho?
  • El material puede ir delante del nombre o con made of: a plastic ruler = a ruler made of plastic.
  • leather /ˈleðə/ (cuero): ea suena /e/, como en head o bread.
