Saber nombrar los objetos que nos rodean (en clase, en el bolso, en el bolsillo) y preguntar qué es algo que no conocemos es de lo primero que necesitamos en un idioma nuevo.

1. Preguntar qué es algo

La pregunta clave es What's this? para algo cercano y What's that? para algo lejano; en plural, What are these / those? Respondemos con It's… o They're…:

What's this? – It's a coin.
What's that? – It's an umbrella.
What are those? – They're scissors.

Si creemos saberlo, preguntamos para confirmar: Is this a pen? – No, it isn't. It's a pencil. La gramática completa está en Demostrativos.

2. a, an o some

Con un objeto usamos a (delante de sonido consonante: a key, a USB) o an (delante de sonido vocal: an earring, an iPad). Con varios, el plural solo o con some: some keys, some tissues. Y recuerda que glasses, sunglasses, headphones y scissors son siempre plurales.

3. Decir lo que llevamos

Para enumerar lo que tenemos usamos I've got…, con comas y and antes del último:

In my bag, I've got a fan, a lipstick, some tissues and a water bottle. That's all!

Recuerda las preposiciones: las cosas están in my bag / pocket / wallet, pero las llaves on a key ring.

4. El vocabulario que hemos visto

En clase En el bolso o el bolsillo
a board /bɔːd/ (whiteboard, blackboard, smart board), a chair /tʃeə/, a desk, a clock, a dictionary, a rubber / an eraser, a sharpener a wallet, a purse, a card holder, keys on a key ring, coins, banknotes, receipts /rɪˈsiːts/, an ID card, a driving licence, tissues, wipes, a lipstick, a hair clip, sweets

En resumen: What's this? – It's a…, What are those? – They're…, y I've got… para enumerar.

Sesión 13jueves 19 de noviembre

What's in your bag?

  • I've got /aɪv ɡɒt/ a pen, some keys… · That's all! / That's it! (eso es todo)
  • In my pocket (bolsillo) · in my bag / purse (bolso, monedero) · in my wallet (cartera)
  • a delante de consonante, an delante de sonido vocal: a pen, an earring, an eraser. Con plurales, some: some keys, some sweets.

Así lo practicamos en clase:

In my bag, I've got a fan, a lipstick, some tissues and a water bottle.
In my wallet, I've got my ID, my driving licence, some coins and a lottery ticket!
In my pocket, I've got some keys on a key ring and an earring. That's all!

  • Para enumerar, comas y and antes del último: a fan, some tissues and a pen.
  • Para preguntar a tu compañero: What's in your bag? · What have you got in your pocket?

Sesión 14martes 24 de noviembre

What's this? Is it a…?

A: What's this?
B: It's a coin. / It's an umbrella.
A: Is this a pen?
B: No, it isn't. It's a pencil.
A: What are those?
B: They're scissors.
A: Are these your keys?
B: Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

  • Para preguntar, be va delante: Is this a pen?, nunca Is a pen this?
  • En la respuesta, ya no repetimos this ni these: usamos it (singular) o they (plural). What's this? – It's a key. · What are these? – They're keys.
  • a delante de sonido consonante, an delante de sonido vocal: a coin, an umbrella, an iPad (la i suena /aɪ/).
  • Pronuncia bien la -ts final de What's e It's: /wɒts/, /ɪts/. Si no suena la s, se entiende what o it.

Sesión 15jueves 26 de noviembre

Whose is this? El genitivo 's

Pregunta Respuesta
Whose is this? (singular) It's Ana's.
Whose are these? (plural) They're Juan's.
Is this / that your key? Yes, it is. · No, it isn't.
Are these / those your glasses? Yes, they are. · No, they aren't.
  • Whose /huːz/ = ¿de quién? Suena igual que Who's (Who is), pero es otra palabra.
  • El 's va detrás del dueño, al revés que en español: Ana's key = la llave de Ana. It's Ana's. = Es de Ana.
  • Sirve para posesiones (Ana's bag) y para relaciones entre personas (Ana's teacher, Juan's father).
  • Is this your key? – Yes, it's mine. · Are these yours? (yours = tuyo, tuyos).

What's it like? Describir objetos

Tamaño Forma Color Material
big, large, small, tiny, long, short, thin, thick, heavy, light round, square, rectangular, flat, oval, triangular black, white, brown, red, yellow, grey… made of plastic, leather, metal, paper, glass, wood
  • What's it like? = ¿Cómo es? (no confundir con Do you like it?). What's it made of? = ¿De qué está hecho?
  • El material puede ir delante del nombre o con made of: a plastic ruler = a ruler made of plastic.
  • leather /ˈleðə/ (cuero): ea suena /e/, como en head o bread.
