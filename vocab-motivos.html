Sesión 1martes 6 de octubre

Pizarra: why do you study English?

  • because I want to travel
  • for my personal development / my job
  • to be a hotel receptionist
  • to improve my knowledge of other languages
  • to travel / to travel around the world
  • to speak and understand English when we travel to other countries
  • to visit my family in the US
  • to speak English with my friends and family
  • to remember past studies
