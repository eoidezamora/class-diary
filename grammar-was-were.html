ArtículoTodo lo visto, explicado y ordenado

El pasado de be tiene solo dos formas, was y were, y la primera ocasión en que lo usamos es para decir dónde y cuándo nacimos.

1. Las formas

Afirmativa Negativa Pregunta
I / he / she / it was wasn't (was not) Was he…?
you / we / they were weren't (were not) Were you…?

Como en el presente, para preguntar ponemos el verbo delante: Were you born in Zamora? – Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.

2. I was born

En inglés «nacer» se dice con be en pasado: I was born. Nunca I borned ni I am born. Detrás, in para lugares, años y meses, y on para una fecha completa:

I was born in Zamora. · I was born in 1995. · I was born on the 15th of April.
Where were you born?

3. Preguntar por otra persona

When was she born? – She was born on the 22nd of August, 1995. · Where was he born? – He was born in London.

En resumen: was con I, he, she, it; were con you, we, they; y I was born in… para el nacimiento.

Sesión 11jueves 12 de noviembre

Where in Spain are you from?

Preguntamos a compañeros de Zamora y de otros países (Colombia, Rusia, Perú) dónde está su ciudad dentro de su país.

A: Where are you from?
B: I'm from Colombia.
A: Where in Colombia? / Is it in the north of Colombia?
B: It's in the east. / Can you spell it, please?

  • Dentro del país: It's in the north / the north-west / the centre of Spain.
  • Cerca de otro lugar: It's next to Portugal. (pegado, al lado) · It's near Madrid. (cerca)
  • Pasado de be: I was · you were · he / she / it was · we / you / they were

Preguntar por otra persona: she / her

Con la actividad W6 practicamos las preguntas en tercera persona:

  • What's her first name / surname / email address / phone number?
  • How do you spell it?
  • When was she born? · Where was she born?
  • she es el sujeto (ella); her, el posesivo (su, de ella): She's a singer. Her name's Dua.
