this, that, these, those

Grammar · Demostrativos: this, that, these, those
Cerca (near) Lejos (far)
Singular this /ðɪs/ · This is a pen. that /ðæt/ · That's a clock.
Plural these /ðiːz/ · These are my keys. those /ðəʊz/ · Those are scissors.
  • this también sirve para presentar a alguien: This is Ana. She's my partner.
  • that también se refiere a algo que ya se ha dicho: That's right! · That's all! · That's it!
  • Contracciones: that's (= that is) se contrae siempre al hablar. This is no se contrae al escribir (nunca this's). Y these are / those are tampoco: se dicen enteros.
  • Ojo con la pronunciación: this /ɪ/ (corta) y these /iː/ (larga). Todas empiezan por /ð/, con la lengua entre los dientes.

What's this? Is it a…?

Function · Identificar objetosGrammar · El verbo be: preguntas y respuestas cortasGrammar · Demostrativos: this, that, these, those

A: What's this?
B: It's a coin. / It's an umbrella.
A: Is this a pen?
B: No, it isn't. It's a pencil.
A: What are those?
B: They're scissors.
A: Are these your keys?
B: Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

  • Para preguntar, be va delante: Is this a pen?, nunca Is a pen this?
  • En la respuesta, ya no repetimos this ni these: usamos it (singular) o they (plural). What's this? – It's a key. · What are these? – They're keys.
  • a delante de sonido consonante, an delante de sonido vocal: a coin, an umbrella, an iPad (la i suena /aɪ/).
  • Pronuncia bien la -ts final de What's e It's: /wɒts/, /ɪts/. Si no suena la s, se entiende what o it.

Plurales

Grammar · a / an / some y los plurales
  • Con un plural nunca usamos a ni an. Decimos el plural solo o con some: some keys, some coins.
  • Algunos plurales terminan en tres consonantes seguidas, y hay que pronunciarlas todas: masks /mɑːsks/, lamps /læmps/, desks /desks/, clocks /klɒks/.
  • Recuerda: glasses, sunglasses, headphones, scissors son siempre plurales: They're my glasses.

Preguntar por otra persona: she / her

Con la actividad W6 practicamos las preguntas en tercera persona:

  • What's her first name / surname / email address / phone number?
  • How do you spell it?
  • When was she born? · Where was she born?
  • she es el sujeto (ella); her, el posesivo (su, de ella): She's a singer. Her name's Dua.
