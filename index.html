Números del 0 al 100, el juego Hit the Ships, Where are you from? en parejas y los ordinales para decir fechas y cumpleaños.

Materiales de la sesiónW7 Where are you from? Nationalities · The British Isles

Qué hicimos

  • Corregimos los deberes (Numbers 0–20) y ampliamos con los números del 20 al 100, contrastando 13 / 30, 14 / 40…
  • Jugamos a Hit the Ships (los barquitos) para repasar letras y números que se confunden.
  • Communicative del libro (A p. 200 / B p. 205): Where's Masako from? – She's from Japan. Where in Japan?
  • W7 Nationalities: la teoría de I'm from… / I was born in… (apartado 4).
  • Números ordinales para las fechas y los cumpleaños, y cómo se leen los años.

Números: -teen o -ty

-teen (acento al final) -ty (acento al principio)
thirTEEN 13 THIRty 30
fourTEEN 14 FORty 40
fifTEEN 15 FIFty 50
  • Ojo con la ortografía: forty (sin u, aunque four la lleva), fifty, eighty.
  • Las decenas con unidades llevan guion: twenty-seven, eighty-four.
  • 100 = a hundred o one hundred.

Pizarra: Hit the Ships

  • each ship = 2 squares · las casillas se dicen letra + número: M18
  • Hit! (tocado) · Sunk! (hundido; sink – sank – sunk) · Nothing! / Miss! (agua)
  • I go! / My turn. · You go! / Your turn.
  • I won / you won (win) · I lost / you lost · a tie /taɪ/ (un empate) · the winner / the loser
  • He's / She's a master / an ace! (¡es un/a crack!)

Pizarra: Where is it?

  • Is it in the north of Colombia? · Where is it in Colombia? – It's in the east.
  • It's near / next to Mongolia.
  • Can you spell it, please? – O-S-A-K-A
  • Zamora is by the river Duero. (junto al río) · the Thames /temz/: la th suena /t/.
  • a dam (una presa) · in the middle west
  • Pasado de be: I was · you were · he / she / it was · we / you / they were**

Fechas: los números ordinales
1st first 2nd second 3rd third 4th fourth
5th fifth 6th sixth 7th seventh 8th eighth
9th ninth 10th tenth 20th twentieth 30th thirtieth
  • Casi todos terminan en -th. Los terminados en 1, 2 y 3 llevan -st, -nd, -rd: 21st, 22nd, 23rd, 31st. Pero 11th, 12th, 13th.
  • Ortografía: fifth, eighth (solo una t), ninth (sin e), twelfth.
  • twenty → twentieth, thirty → thirtieth: la y cambia a ie.
  • Para decir la fecha: on the* 27th of* November → on the twenty-seventh of November.
  • Los meses van con mayúscula: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December.

Cómo se leen los años

  • 1995 → nineteen ninety-five (de dos en dos)
  • 2002 → two thousand and two (o twenty oh two)
  • I was born in 1995. · I was born on the 15th of April.
